## 挥手

最终素材：waving-smooth.png，1536×1024，4列×2行，8个姿势。

生成提示词：

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production 2D desktop-character animation sprite sheet. Input image 1 is the exact XiaoG white dragon girl identity and outfit reference. Input image 2 is the original 4-pose wave reference. Create ONE new crisp animation sheet on a genuinely transparent RGBA background, 4 columns by 2 rows, exactly EIGHT full-body figures, read left to right then top to bottom. Generous transparent gaps, no character crosses a cell, no labels/grid/borders/shadows/background/text. All eight figures have identical height, scale, front-facing body, fixed feet and fixed wing/tail silhouette. Preserve the exact gray-white hair, lavender eyes, white ribbed dragon horns, single ahoge, left-temple knot ornament appearing on viewer-right with tassel, pointy ears, white Chinese-style dress, pale lavender wings and curled white tail. Match the thin crisp original linework and restrained shading, NOT pixel art, NOT 3D, no circuits. This sheet is eight consecutive key poses for a small friendly wave, ONLY her right arm (viewer-left) moves: 1 neutral hand at hip relaxed, 2 hand starts lifting just above hip, 3 elbow gently bends hand around lower chest level, 4 hand reaches shoulder level, 5 open palm beside cheek at mouth height, 6 palm remains beside cheek and fingers give a tiny wave while both eyes remain open, 7 same palm position with one gentle wink, 8 palm returns to the open-eyed pose of frame 5. Do not jump or move feet. Intermediate frames 2-4 are critical; show clearly distinct gradual arm elevation, not eight copies of the final pose. Every figure has exactly TWO hands total, one waving and one relaxed; no extra limbs or ghost silhouettes. Head remains in the same position, with at most a tiny friendly tilt in frames 5-8. Aim for a high-resolution canvas about 3072x2048 so each figure retains detailed eyes, hair knot and dress embroidery. Keep the original character proportions and visual identity.
