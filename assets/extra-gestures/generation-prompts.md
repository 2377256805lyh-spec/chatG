# 肢体动作原画提示词

使用内置 ImageGen 编辑参考 `thinking/pose-00.png`，请求透明背景；未使用 CLI 或外部生成接口。原始输出保留在 `raw/`，对齐图在 `aligned/`；安装版只使用固定身体底图和独立肢体帧。

人物原画最终仅提供手臂、袖口与手掌区域，其余区域继续沿用原人物。每个动作 6 个手绘关键姿势，肩、肘、腕、指端的定位和裁切区域保存在 `regions.json`。

## shy

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: production 2D chibi character animation KEYFRAME SPRITE SHEET on genuine transparent RGBA. Input image 1 is the exact white-haired lavender-eyed dragon-girl identity and costume reference. Create ONE crisp large sheet with exactly 6 complete full-body copies in a regular 3-column by 2-row equal grid, chronological left to right then next row. Each character same front-facing camera, same head/face/neck, body, hair, gray horns, lavender wings, white rose-knot hairpin, long white qipao dress, detached bell sleeves, legs/shoes and curved white tail as the reference. Keep head, torso, feet identical in placement and scale across all six cells. Only arm/hand/sleeve poses change. Action: shy, hands lifted toward BOTH cheeks, cute natural anatomy with relaxed wrists. Frame 1: exact neutral arms lowered diagonally. Frame 2: elbows bend, both hands relaxed next to waist. Frame 3: both hands lift to chest, elbows move gently outward. Frame 4: both hands lift just beneath left/right cheeks, backs of curled fingers toward viewer, palms angled toward own cheeks, NOT a wave. Frame 5: same hands cheek pose with a subtle inward wrist curl, fingertips delicately touching cheeks. Frame 6: hands slightly below cheeks in the early lowering movement, elbows naturally relax. Arm and bell-sleeve folds should be redrawn naturally per frame, flexible rather than a rigid rotated cutout. Five fingers maximum, petite elegant chibi hands, anatomically plausible. Face invariant: same eyes, same jaw, same small mouth, do not cover either eye or mouth, no facial deformation; NO head turn, NO body turn. Consistent fine dark lavender-gray outlines, pearl white and lavender flat anime shading with matching soft highlights, clean high resolution. Full body with generous transparent margin in every cell, at least 20px blank gutters, no overlaps between cells. Exact aligned fixed size poses, no alternate redesign. No text, numbers, borders, watermark, props, hearts or particles; add decorations separately later. This is a carefully coordinated animation sheet, not six unrelated character illustrations.
